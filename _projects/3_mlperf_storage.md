Led MangoBoost's MLPerf Storage benchmark submissions.

In **v2.0** (2025), Mango StorageBoost sustained line-rate throughput over a 400G
Ethernet fabric, delivering 6.2x GPU scalability and 1.57–2.05x higher throughput
per unit of network bandwidth over competing solutions, and outperforming NVIDIA
BlueField-3 in both NVMe/TCP and NVMe/RDMA configurations.

In **v1.0** (2024), the submission averaged 2.7x (up to 4.7x) higher performance
than other submitters on ResNet50 and 3D-UNet.
