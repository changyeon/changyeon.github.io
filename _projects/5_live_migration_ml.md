VM live migration is the foundation of seamless cloud service management, but
predicting its key performance metrics is notoriously difficult due to its complex
behavior. This project proposed a machine learning approach to live migration
modeling. Trained on 40,000 VM live migration records, the model showed 2 to 5
times better prediction accuracy than the state-of-the-art analytical model.

Published at SoCC'17.
